ChokeSafe

Pediatric Food Safety Guide

Based on the L2P4011 Physical Risk Assessment frameworks.

High Risk Geometric Limits

Objects with rigid, non-compressible spherical or oval profiles measuring between 1.6cm and 4.3cm fall directly inside dangerous airway-blocking parameters.

Hazard Intrinsic Qualities

Risk parameters exponentially increase when a food combines structural firmness with a slippery surface, or has a texture that swells when exposed to moisture.

Risk Classification Framework

High Risk FoodHazard AttributesMandatory Modifications
Whole Grapes, Cherry TomatoesSpherical or oval geometries perfectly match airway sizes; slippery outer skins.Must be quartered lengthwise. Never serve whole.
Hot Dogs, SausagesCylindrical shape. When cut into round discs, they form an airtight seal.Slice lengthwise into semi-cylinders, then into small fragments.
Hard Candies, Cough DropsUnyielding and firm. Resists compression and does not dissolve quickly.Strictly avoid for children under 4. Grind nuts into powders.
Marshmallows, CaramelsPliable and sticky. Expands and molds to fill the airway.Do not serve whole. Melt or chop into tiny shards <0.5cm.
Medium Risk FoodHazard AttributesMitigation Protocols
Raw Carrots, ApplesFirm, hard, crisp. Breaks into sharp chunks that can wedge.Steam, boil, or cook until soft. Or grate into thin shreds.
Tough Meat & PoultryFibrous, dense, difficult to chew. Often swallowed whole.Mince finely or shred along grain lines into tiny pieces <0.5cm.
Dried FruitsLeathery, tough, cohesive. Swells and becomes stickier.Chop finely or rehydrate in warm water until soft.
Safe / Low Risk FoodHazard AttributesPreparation Status
Purees, YogurtsFluid or blended. Bypasses airways smoothly.Ready to serve. Ensure no solid chunks.
Soft Bananas, AvocadosSoft, yielding, easy to smash with tongue pressure.Cut into finger-food lengths or mash with a fork.
Dissolving Rice MeltsDissolves rapidly on contact with saliva.Ready to serve. Keep in airtight packaging.
